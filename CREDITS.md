# Credits

CamRush bundles free art so it works fully offline.

- **Kenney.nl** game assets (Shooting Gallery, Tappy Plane, Space Shooter Remastered, Puzzle Pack 1 and 2, Animal Pack Remastered, Food Kit and Platformer Kit rendered to sprites). Licensed **CC0 1.0** (public domain). https://kenney.nl/assets
- Hand and face tracking: **MediaPipe** (Apache 2.0).

Sprites live in `src/assets/*.js` as base64 data URIs. `tools/pack.py` and `tools/assets.json` regenerate them from the Kenney packs.
